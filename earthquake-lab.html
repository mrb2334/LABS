Earthquake Lab

Shear-building model with bilinear story springs (post-yield stiffness set by the construction type), solved by Newmark-β with Newton iteration. Stiffness = 9 columns × 12EI/h³, with E, section and joint/cracking factors taken from the construction-type library (steel, concrete, timber, masonry…); mass = floor load adjusted for the frame’s self-weight. The 3D view shows the computed floor displacements, not a canned shake.
