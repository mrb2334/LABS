Simulations

Each simulation opens in its own browser tab. Greyed-out boxes are on the roadmap and will light up as they are added.

Built to teach, built to be checked

The goal is understanding, so every result can be inspected and verified.

Real solvers

Newmark-β time integration with Newton iteration, bilinear hysteresis, P–Δ effects and vertical collapse – not a canned animation.

Self-verifying

Energy balance, analytic benchmarks and convergence tests run against the solver, and the Energy panel shows where every joule goes.

Runs in your browser

One HTML file per simulation, computed locally, with WebGPU 3D where your browser supports it.
